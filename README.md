## About AfriScholar

Finding scholarships can be challenging. Information about educational funding is often scattered across university websites, government portals, international organizations, and other platforms.

AfriScholar aims to make this process easier by bringing scholarship opportunities together in one accessible place.

We want to help students spend less time searching for opportunities and more time preparing for their educational journeys.

## What We Offer

AfriScholar is designed to help students:

- **Discover Scholarships:** Explore scholarships, fellowships, and educational funding opportunities.
- **Search Opportunities:** Find relevant opportunities using keywords.
- **Filter Scholarships:** Narrow down opportunities based on degree level, country of study, and funding type.
- **Explore Scholarship Details:** Review eligibility requirements, funding benefits, application deadlines, and other important information.
- **Access Application Links:** Connect directly to official scholarship application websites.
- **Stay Connected:** Subscribe to receive information about scholarship opportunities.

## How AfriScholar Works

Finding an opportunity through AfriScholar is designed to be straightforward.

**1. Discover Opportunities**

Browse available scholarships and explore opportunities that match your academic interests.

**2. Search and Filter**

Use the search and filtering features to identify scholarships based on your preferred degree level, country of study, or funding type.

**3. Review Scholarship Requirements**

Read the scholarship description carefully, including eligibility criteria, funding benefits, application deadlines, and other requirements.

**4. Apply Through Official Platforms**

Follow the application link to visit the official scholarship provider's website and complete the application process.

**5. Stay Connected**

Subscribe to AfriScholar to stay informed about scholarship opportunities.

## Who Can Benefit from AfriScholar?

AfriScholar is primarily intended for African students and graduates seeking educational opportunities.

Our intended audience includes:

- Undergraduate students.
- Master's degree applicants.
- PhD and doctoral candidates.
- Recent university graduates.
- Students seeking fully funded scholarships.
- Individuals interested in international fellowships and academic grants.

## Our Vision

We believe that access to educational opportunities should not be limited by a student's ability to find information.

Through AfriScholar, we aim to contribute to a more accessible educational landscape where African students can discover opportunities, pursue their academic ambitions, and build better futures.

We are committed to continuously improving the platform and making scholarship information more accessible to the people who need it.

## Important Information

AfriScholar is an independent scholarship discovery platform. We provide scholarship information to help students identify potential educational opportunities.

- AfriScholar is not a scholarship provider.
- We do not award scholarships or make admission decisions.
- Scholarship applications are submitted through the respective official providers.
- Scholarship availability, eligibility requirements, benefits, and deadlines may change.
- Students are encouraged to verify all information through the official scholarship provider before applying.

## Technology

AfriScholar is built using:

- PHP
- MySQL / MariaDB
- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- PDO for database connectivity

The platform uses a PHP-based administration dashboard to manage scholarship information and subscriber records.
